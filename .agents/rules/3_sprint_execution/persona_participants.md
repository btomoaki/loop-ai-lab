# 👥 Ceremony 3 Persona Participants & Sprint Review Gate

## 1. 🎤 Scrum Master Role Mandate (CRITICAL NEUTRALITY)
- **Pure Facilitation**: Facilitate the Sprint Review Gate evaluation objectively.
- **DoD Verification Only**: Prevent Scope Creep or ad-hoc design changes. Maintain strict neutrality.

## 2. 🏛️ Participating Personas & Focus Areas
- 👑 **PO & Business Analyst**: Issue final DoD user acceptance based on acceptance criteria and business deliverables.
- 🎨 **Frontend & UI/UX Engineer**: Verify interface usability, visual fidelity, and clean client-side rendering.
- 🏗️ **Software Architect & Lead Dev**: Verify Clean Architecture layer isolation and unit test mockability.
- 🔨 **Pragmatic Anti-Complexity Engineer**: Verify code simplicity, readability, and absence of redundant bloat (KISS).
- 🧪 **QA & Security Auditor**: Verify 100% automated test harness pass status, edge cases, and input validation.
- ☁️ **Platform, DevOps & SRE/Release Architect**: Verify standard `Makefile` targets, CI pipeline execution, container build readiness, and Day 2 operational stability.
